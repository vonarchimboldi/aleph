Represent it. Preserve it. Count it.

Edition: The Algorithm Design Manual, third edition. Relative study days; no calendar dates assigned.

Week 1 · Refresh and diagnose

Days1–7 cover the shared core. Each day has review exposition, two worked examples, a five-question diagnostic, worked feedback, a repair prompt, a fresh check, a big-picture summary and a mind map. Available now.

Days8–14 revisit the same scope through new inputs, implementation comparisons and compact exam-style combinations. Use Week1 evidence to adjust emphasis. Daily material not yet authored.

Week 3 · Review and remedy

Days15–21 target confirmed errors, then use delayed independent checks and a mixed final review. Daily material will be selected from the prior diagnostics; it is not yet authored.

What belongs in the intersection?

Skiena is broader than the exam. The core reading comes from analysis, elementary structures, sorting, hashing, traversal and weighted paths. The official CMI MSc Data Science entrance syllabus asks for simple pseudocode interpretation rather than explicitly listing the entire GATE data-structures curriculum. We therefore label CMI-oriented trace questions separately instead of claiming that every advanced structure is an explicit CMI topic.

The programming supplement supplies Python-specific behavior required by GATE DA. All exposition and problems here are original reading companions, not copied book exercises or claimed past examination questions.

Seven-day review route

  1. Day 1 · Analysis, Python traces and search

    Separate exact counts from growth bounds, and explain why a search interval shrinks.

    ADM 3e Chapters 1–2; Chapter 5 binary search

  2. Day 2 · Arrays, linked lists, stacks and queues

    Choose a representation from its operations and protect its state invariant.

    ADM 3e Chapter 3: elementary data structures

  3. Day 3 · Dictionaries: hashing, binary search trees and traversal

    Distinguish expected costs from guarantees, and membership lookup from ordered queries.

    ADM 3e Chapter 3; Chapter 6: hashing

  4. Day 4 · Elementary sorting and the meaning of stability

    Trace selection, insertion and bubble sort using a stated implementation and count the requested operation.

    ADM 3e Chapter 4; Python implementations supplement the exam list

  5. Day 5 · Divide and conquer, mergesort, quicksort and heaps

    Explain partition/merge invariants and choose a recurrence from the code’s work.

    ADM 3e Chapters 4–5; Chapter 4 priority queues

  6. Day 6 · Graph representations, BFS and DFS

    Make graph traces deterministic and distinguish reachability, traversal order and unweighted distance.

    ADM 3e Chapter 7: graph traversal

  7. Day 7 · Shortest paths and the week’s cumulative check

    Choose a shortest-path method from its edge assumptions, then revisit earlier invariants.

    ADM 3e Chapter 8; cumulative Chapters 2–7

A daily diagnostic you can interpret

Two multi-select questions and three short-response questions, ten marks total, about25 minutes plus checking. Record reasoning, attempt status, confidence and help. Answers and consolidation appear after recording a local attempt. The original attempt is kept separately from corrections; upload the export in the subject workspace for an Aleph submission and its existing AI feedback flow. A local unlock is not a server submission.

Classify misses as: problem/model interpretation, representation choice, invariant/state update, boundary/equality case, exact-count versus asymptotic reasoning, or unsupported conclusion. Diagnose the first broken step rather than calling an entire topic “weak” from one mistake.

The big picture

Changing the representation changes the available cheap operations. An invariant explains why those operations answer the question. Counting the actual work then explains the running time.
