Calculus · Round 2 · Study days 11–20

200 source-linked problems. Ten practice days.

Spend 60 minutes attempting the day’s packet, then submit your answers to unlock worked solutions and review your work. Each packet has 20 questions. Some are long proofs: the packet is a practice bank, not a promise that all 20 fit in an hour. Stop at 60 minutes; record anything untouched as unattempted.

How to use a day

  1. Open the source PDFs and find the listed question numbers before starting. PDF page numbers include cover pages.
  2. Start the timer. Write your work on paper or in a separate document. First skim for questions you can begin; return to harder ones. Record help when used.
  3. At 60 minutes, save the first attempt and submit your answers. Aleph must confirm that they were saved on the server before it unlocks the worked solutions. Then spend about 30 minutes checking and correcting your work.
  4. For each difficulty, write the first wrong or missing step, explain the repair, then close the solution and try again. Revisit the problem after two days; use a new variant later to check transfer.
  5. Export the record and upload it with your handwritten work through Aleph → Subjects → Calculus: 30-day review → the matching Round 2 day.

Drafts stay on this browser until you press Submit answers. A successful submission saves your original work on the server and unlocks only that day’s solutions. Submission does not verify mastery.
