Sources, scope and review

The notes link every historical question to its official paper and preserve published keys, including flagged ambiguities. Coverage is DA2024–2026 plus 21 selected CS questions; it is not every historical CS/IT question. Normal-form teaching in this first-pass pack runs through BCNF.

Exercise calculations were checked from question inputs. Explanations, source answers and PDF layout received author review. This is not an independent editorial certification.
