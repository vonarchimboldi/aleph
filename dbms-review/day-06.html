Declare what one fact row means

A fact table records measurements at a specified grain, such as one product sold at one store on one date. Dimension tables describe product, store and time. Changing the grain changes what can be added without double counting. A star schema connects dimensions directly to the fact; a snowflake design further normalizes dimension hierarchies. Neither name determines the correct grain for you.

A hierarchy orders detail levels, such as City→State→Country→ALL. Roll-up moves toward coarser groups; drill-down moves toward detail. Slicing fixes a dimension value, while dicing selects a subregion using conditions or sets. Pivoting changes the presentation orientation, not the underlying total. State the hierarchy instead of assuming that every attribute is naturally comparable.

Two different things are counted in cube questions

A cuboid is one selected aggregation level for each dimension. With d dimensions and only detail/ALL choices, there are2ᵈ cuboids. If dimension i has Lᵢ usable levels including ALL, and one level per independent hierarchy is chosen, the number of cuboids is the product of the Lᵢ. This is not the number of rows in a fully materialized cube.

For two dimensions with m and n distinct detail values, when every combination occurs and the only levels are detail and ALL, GROUP BY CUBE returns mn detail rows, m first-dimension subtotals, n second-dimension subtotals and one grand total. The row count is(m+1)(n+1). Missing combinations and filters can change row counts even though the cuboid types remain the same.

Not every measure can be added in every direction

Revenue and units sold are typically additive across independent dimensions. An inventory snapshot may be additive across stores but not across dates: adding today’s stock to yesterday’s stock does not give current stock. A rate or average cannot normally be summed. To combine averages correctly, keep sufficient statistics such as sum and count.

Distributive aggregates such as SUM and COUNT can be combined using the same fixed aggregate on partitions. Algebraic aggregates such as AVG can be reconstructed from a fixed collection like SUM and COUNT. An exact MEDIAN is holistic under the conventional classification: fixed-size partition summaries do not generally determine the exact combined median. These categories describe how much information aggregation must retain.

Two worked examples

Cube rows are not cube types

Product has2 values and Region has3, every combination occurs. Only detail and ALL levels are used. Count cuboids and GROUP BY CUBE rows.

There are2²=4 cuboids. Their row counts are6 detail rows,2 product subtotals,3 region subtotals and1 grand total:12 rows. The four aggregation types and twelve output rows answer different questions.

Keep sum and count

One group has10 records averaging20; another has30 averaging40. What is the combined average?

Recover totals:10×20=200 and30×40=1200. Combined average=(200+1200)/(10+30)=35. Taking(20+40)/2=30 would give the groups equal weight even though their record counts differ.
