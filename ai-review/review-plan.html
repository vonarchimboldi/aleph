A review week’s sequence

  1. Closed-notes mixed diagnostic and confidence predictions.
  2. Repair the first high-value gap; solve a fresh contrastive pair.
  3. Repair the second gap; explain the method choice aloud.
  4. Mixed application: identify methods without topic labels.
  5. Timed exam-style problems; distinguish knowledge from speed errors.
  6. Delayed retrieval of the week’s repairs; include older material.
  7. Student-led review: show evidence, unresolved gaps and a proposed next plan.
