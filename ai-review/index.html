Artificial Intelligence · GATE DA 2027

How we will teach this material: the Skycak tutor skill and sources. Unit1 now uses this approach. The remaining units retain their first-pass format and need the same expansion.

Seven teaching units covering search, logic and probabilistic reasoning. This is the AI section, separate from Machine Learning.

Start with a short diagnostic. A lack of prior exposure calls for teaching; it is not a failure to revise. Use three sessions per week as a proposed allocation within existing study capacity, replacing slots rather than adding workload automatically. At that rate these seven units take approximately three weeks, followed by a mixed assessment and targeted repair.

  1. Uninformed search · AIMA 3
  2. Informed search: greedy and A* · AIMA 3
  3. Adversarial search: minimax and alpha–beta · AIMA 5
  4. Propositional and predicate logic · AIMA 7–9
  5. Conditional independence and Bayesian networks · AIMA 12–13
  6. Exact inference through variable elimination · AIMA 13
  7. Approximate inference through sampling · AIMA 13

How understanding will be checked

For every unit: record an original attempt, help used, written reasoning and a specific difficulty. Use a fresh question after feedback, then a delayed check and an unfamiliar variation. Track these separately from PDF uploads and self-ratings. The dashboard requires mentor-confirmed independent evidence before describing a topic as demonstrated.

Sources and boundaries

Official GATE 2027 DA syllabus; AIMA authors’ contents and resources; Berkeley CS188 course text. Broader AI topics such as reinforcement learning, NLP and robotics are outside this specific syllabus list. The seven units are a first pass, not a complete mock-exam programme.
