Questions 1-6 - Weekly ISI-Style Review
1. Indicators: isolated edges and a second moment
IndicatorsPair dependence

In (G(n,p)), let (X) count components consisting of exactly two vertices joined by one edge. (a) Derive (E[X]). (b) Classify pairs of candidate edges by overlap and derive an exact expression for (E[X(X-1)]). (c) State the resulting second-moment lower bound for (P(X>0)).

2. Conditional expectation: random environment
Tower propertyPrediction

Let (P\sim Beta(a,b)); given (P), let (X_1,\dots,X_n,Y) be iid Bernoulli((P)). With (S=\sum X_i), derive (E[Y\mid S]), (Var(Y\mid S)), and (Cov(X_1,Y)). Explain which dependence comes only from mixing over (P).

3. Order statistics: exponential race and maximum limit
SpacingsExtreme values

For iid (Exp(1)) observations: (a) identify the laws of the first two spacings; (b) compute (E[X_{(k)}]); (c) prove (X_{(n)}-\log n) converges to the Gumbel distribution.

4. Estimation: a nonregular endpoint
MLESupportAsymptotics

For iid (U(0,\theta)): (a) derive the MLE; (b) construct an unbiased estimator from the maximum; (c) find its variance; (d) derive the limiting law of (n(\theta-X_{(n)})/\theta) and explain why regular Fisher-information heuristics fail.

5. UMP testing: Poisson upper boundary
MLRRandomization

For iid (Pois(\lambda)), test (H_0:\lambda\le\lambda_0) against (H_1:\lambda>\lambda_0). Derive the UMP rejection direction from the likelihood ratio, calibrate an exact level-(alpha) randomized boundary using (S=\sum X_i), and write the power function.

6. Regression repair: leverage and heteroskedasticity
Regression repairDiagnostics

In full-rank linear regression: (a) prove (H) is a projection and (sum h_{ii}=p); (b) derive (Var(\hat\beta\mid X)) when (Var(\varepsilon\mid X)=\Omega); (c) derive the leave-one-out residual (e_i/(1-h_{ii})); (d) explain why a high-leverage point can be influential despite a modest ordinary residual.
