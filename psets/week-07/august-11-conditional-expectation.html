Core Pattern

Condition on the variable that freezes the complicated part. Compute inside first, then use the tower property or total variance.

\[E[X]=E(E[X\mid Y]),\quad Var(X)=E(Var(X\mid Y))+Var(E[X\mid Y]).\]
Problems 1-5 - Concept Builders
1. Random coin

Let (P\sim U(0,1)), and (X\mid P\sim Bin(n,P)). Find (E[X]).

Solution

(E[X\mid P]=nP), hence (E[X]=nE[P]=n/2).

2. Conditional predictor

If (E[X\mid Y]=2Y+1), find the mean-square optimal predictor of (X) based on (Y).

Solution

The conditional expectation itself: (2Y+1).

3. Random sum mean

Let (N) be independent of iid (Z_i) with mean (mu). Find (E[\sum_{i=1}^N Z_i]).

Solution

Condition on (N): the inner mean is (N\mu). Therefore the answer is (mu E[N]).

4. Poisson thinning

If (N\sim Pois(\lambda)) and (X\mid N\sim Bin(N,p)), find (E[X]) and (Var(X)).

Solution

The mean is (p\lambda). Total variance gives (p(1-p)\lambda+p^2\lambda=p\lambda).

5. Posterior mean

For (P\sim Beta(a,b)) and (X\mid P\sim Bin(n,P)), find (E[P\mid X=x]).

Solution

The posterior is (Beta(a+x,b+n-x)), so the mean is ((a+x)/(a+b+n)).

Problems 6-8 - Integration Problems
6. Random sum variance

Under Problem 3, with (Var(Z_i)=\sigma^2), derive the variance of the random sum.

Solution

Given (N), variance is (N\sigma^2) and mean is (N\mu). Thus (Var(S)=\sigma^2E[N]+\mu^2Var(N)).

7. Exchangeable Bernoulli covariance

Given (P), let (X_1,X_2) be independent Bernoulli((P)). Show (Cov(X_1,X_2)=Var(P)).

Solution

(E[X_1X_2]=E[P^2]) and (E[X_i]=E[P]), so covariance is (E[P^2]-E[P]^2).

8. Conditional centering

Prove (E[(X-E[X\mid Y])g(Y)]=0) for every bounded (g).

Solution

Condition on (Y): the inner expectation is (g(Y)(E[X\mid Y]-E[X\mid Y])=0); apply tower.

Problems 9-10 - ISI-Style Challenge
9. Stopped fair walk

Let (S_n=\sum_{i=1}^n Z_i), where iid (Z_i) have mean (0), and let bounded (N) depend only on past increments. Show (E[S_N]=0).

Solution

Write (S_N=\sum_i Z_i1_{N\ge i}). The indicator is determined before (Z_i), so each term has expectation zero.

10. Mixture variance identification

Suppose (X\mid Y\sim N(Y,1)) and (Y\sim N(0,\tau^2)). Find the distribution of (X) and identify both total-variance components.

Solution

Write (X=Y+\varepsilon) with independent (N(0,1)) noise. Thus (X\sim N(0,\tau^2+1)); components are (1) and ( au^2).
