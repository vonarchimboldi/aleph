Core Pattern

Condition on the variable that makes the remaining experiment familiar.

\[E[X]=E[E[X\mid Y]],\qquad \operatorname{Var}(X)=E[\operatorname{Var}(X\mid Y)]+\operatorname{Var}(E[X\mid Y]).\]
Problems 1-5 - Concept Builders
1. Random success probability
Week 5Diagnostic

Given \(P\), \(X\sim\operatorname{Bin}(n,P)\). If \(E[P]=q\), find \(E[X]\).

Solution

\(E[X\mid P]=nP\). Taking expectation gives \(E[X]=nq\).

2. Conditional Poisson count
Week 5Diagnostic

Given \(\Lambda\), \(X\sim\operatorname{Poisson}(\Lambda)\). Find \(E[X]\) and \(\operatorname{Var}(X)\) using \(E[\Lambda]\) and \(\operatorname{Var}(\Lambda)\).

Solution

Both conditional mean and variance equal \(\Lambda\). Thus \(E[X]=E[\Lambda]\) and \(\operatorname{Var}(X)=E[\Lambda]+\operatorname{Var}(\Lambda)\).

3. Random sample size
Week 5Diagnostic

Given \(N\), \(X_1,\ldots,X_N\) are iid with mean \(\mu\), independent of \(N\). Find \(E[\sum_{i=1}^N X_i]\).

Solution

Conditioning on \(N\) gives conditional mean \(N\mu\). Hence the answer is \(\mu E[N]\).

4. Two-stage die
Week 5Diagnostic

A fair die gives \(Y\). Then a fair coin is tossed \(Y\) times. Find the expected number of heads.

Solution

Given \(Y\), the mean is \(Y/2\). Therefore \(E[X]=E[Y]/2=(7/2)/2=7/4\).

5. Conditional variance zero
Week 5Diagnostic

If \(X=g(Y)\), what is \(\operatorname{Var}(X\mid Y)\)?

Solution

Once \(Y\) is known, \(g(Y)\) is fixed. Therefore the conditional variance is zero.

Problems 6-8 - Integration Problems
6. Beta-Bernoulli variance
Week 5Diagnostic

Given \(P\), \(X\sim\operatorname{Bernoulli}(P)\). Show that the unconditional variance depends only on \(q=E[P]\).

Solution

Total variance gives \(E[P(1-P)]+\operatorname{Var}(P)=E[P]-E[P^2]+E[P^2]-q^2=q(1-q)\).

7. Geometric stopping reward
Week 5Diagnostic

Trials continue until the first success, with success probability \(p\). Each failed trial costs \(c\) and the success earns \(r\). Find expected net reward.

Solution

The number of failures before success has mean \((1-p)/p\). Expected net reward is \(r-c(1-p)/p\).

8. Random-effects covariance
Week 5Diagnostic

Given \(P\), \(X_1,X_2\) are conditionally independent Bernoulli(\(P\)). Find \(\operatorname{Cov}(X_1,X_2)\).

Solution

\(E[X_1X_2]=E[P^2]\), while both means equal \(E[P]\). Hence the covariance is \(\operatorname{Var}(P)\).

Problems 9-10 - ISI-Style Challenge
9. Compound sum variance
Week 5Diagnostic

Let \(S=\sum_{i=1}^N X_i\), where \(N\) is independent of iid \(X_i\) with mean \(\mu\) and variance \(\sigma^2\). Find \(\operatorname{Var}(S)\).

Solution

Given \(N\), mean is \(N\mu\) and variance is \(N\sigma^2\). Thus \(\operatorname{Var}(S)=\sigma^2E[N]+\mu^2\operatorname{Var}(N)\).

10. Posterior predictive coin
Week 5Diagnostic

A coin is selected: with probability \(1/2\) it has head probability \(1/4\), otherwise \(3/4\). After observing one head, find the probability the next toss is a head.

Solution

Posterior weights are proportional to \(1/4\) and \(3/4\), so they are \(1/4,3/4\). Predictive probability is \((1/4)(1/4)+(3/4)(3/4)=5/8\).
