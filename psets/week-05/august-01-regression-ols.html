Core Pattern

OLS makes the residual orthogonal to every fitted design direction.

\[X^\top(y-X\hat\beta)=0,\qquad \hat\beta=(X^\top X)^{-1}X^\top y\ \text{when full rank holds}.\]
Problems 1-5 - Concept Builders
1. Centered slope
Week 5Diagnostic

In simple regression with intercept, express the OLS slope using centered data.

Solution

\(\hat\beta_1=\sum_i(x_i-\bar x)(y_i-\bar y)/\sum_i(x_i-\bar x)^2\).

2. Mean-point property
Week 5Diagnostic

Show where the fitted simple-regression line passes.

Solution

The intercept equation gives \(\hat\beta_0=\bar y-\hat\beta_1\bar x\), so the line passes through \((\bar x,\bar y)\).

3. Residual sum
Week 5Diagnostic

With an intercept in the model, what is \(\sum_i e_i\)?

Solution

The intercept column is the all-ones vector. Residual orthogonality to this column gives \(\sum_i e_i=0\).

4. Scaling the response
Week 5Diagnostic

If every response is multiplied by \(c\), what happens to all OLS coefficients?

Solution

Linearity of \((X^\top X)^{-1}X^\top y\) means every coefficient is multiplied by \(c\).

5. Shifting a predictor
Week 5Diagnostic

In simple regression with intercept, replace \(x_i\) by \(x_i+a\). What happens to slope and fitted values?

Solution

The centered predictor is unchanged, so slope and fitted values remain unchanged; the intercept adjusts by \(-a\hat\beta_1\).

Problems 6-8 - Integration Problems
6. Through-origin fit
Week 5Diagnostic

For model \(y_i=\beta x_i+e_i\) without intercept, find the OLS slope.

Solution

Minimize \(\sum(y_i-\beta x_i)^2\). The normal equation gives \(\hat\beta=\sum x_iy_i/\sum x_i^2\).

7. Hat-matrix identities
Week 5Diagnostic

For full-rank \(X\), define \(H=X(X^\top X)^{-1}X^\top\). State two key algebraic properties.

Solution

\(H^\top=H\) and \(H^2=H\): it is a symmetric idempotent projection onto the column space of \(X\).

8. Duplicate predictor
Week 5Diagnostic

What happens if a design matrix includes the same predictor twice?

Solution

The columns are linearly dependent, so \(X^\top X\) is singular and individual duplicate coefficients are not uniquely identified, although fitted values may be.

Problems 9-10 - ISI-Style Challenge
9. Constrained coefficients
Week 5Diagnostic

Minimize \(\|y-X\beta\|^2\) subject to \(R\beta=r\). What geometric fact replaces unconstrained residual orthogonality?

Solution

At the constrained optimum, \(X^\top(y-X\hat\beta)\) need not be zero; it lies in the row space of \(R\), balancing the Lagrange-multiplier constraint.

10. Omitted-variable direction
Week 5Diagnostic

True model is \(Y=\beta_0+\beta_1X+\gamma Z+\varepsilon\), but \(Z\) is omitted. If \(\gamma>0\) and \(\operatorname{Cov}(X,Z)>0\), what is the direction of slope bias?

Solution

The simple-regression slope has expectation \(\beta_1+\gamma\operatorname{Cov}(X,Z)/\operatorname{Var}(X)\). Both extra factors are positive, so the bias is upward.
