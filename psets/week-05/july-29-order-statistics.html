Problems 1-5 - Concept Builders
1. Uniform maximum CDF
Week 5Diagnostic

For iid \(U(0,1)\) variables, find the CDF and density of \(M=X_{(n)}\).

Solution

For \(0\le x\le1\), \(F_M(x)=x^n\), so \(f_M(x)=nx^{n-1}\).

2. Uniform minimum mean
Week 5Diagnostic

For iid \(U(0,1)\), find \(E[X_{(1)}]\).

Solution

The minimum has density \(n(1-x)^{n-1}\), or Beta\((1,n)\), whose mean is \(1/(n+1)\).

3. Exponential minimum
Week 5Diagnostic

If \(X_i\) are iid exponential with rate \(\lambda\), identify the law of \(X_{(1)}\).

Solution

\(P(X_{(1)}>x)=e^{-n\lambda x}\), so the minimum is exponential with rate \(n\lambda\).

4. Median density
Week 5Diagnostic

Write the density of \(X_{(2)}\) for three iid continuous observations with CDF \(F\) and density \(f\).

Solution

The kth-order formula gives \(f_{(2)}(x)=6F(x)[1-F(x)]f(x)\).

5. Range support
Week 5Diagnostic

For iid \(U(0,1)\), what is the support of \((X_{(1)},X_{(n)})=(u,v)\)?

Solution

The ordered endpoints must satisfy \(0<u<v<1\).

Problems 6-8 - Integration Problems
6. Joint min-max density
Week 5Diagnostic

Find the joint density of the minimum \(U\) and maximum \(V\) of \(n\) iid \(U(0,1)\) variables.

Solution

Choose observations at \(u,v\) and put the remaining \(n-2\) between them: \(f_{U,V}(u,v)=n(n-1)(v-u)^{n-2}\), \(0<u<v<1\).

7. Expected uniform range
Week 5Diagnostic

Find \(E[X_{(n)}-X_{(1)}]\) for iid \(U(0,1)\).

Solution

The maximum mean is \(n/(n+1)\), the minimum mean is \(1/(n+1)\). Their difference is \((n-1)/(n+1)\).

8. Quantile transform
Week 5Diagnostic

If \(U_{(k)}\) is the kth order statistic from iid uniforms and \(F\) is continuous and strictly increasing, express the kth order statistic from distribution \(F\).

Solution

Apply the quantile map to each ordered uniform: \(X_{(k)}=F^{-1}(U_{(k)})\).

Problems 9-10 - ISI-Style Challenge
9. Scaled exponential minimum
Week 5Diagnostic

For iid exponential(\(\lambda\)) observations, find the distribution of \(nX_{(1)}\).

Solution

\(X_{(1)}\sim\operatorname{Exp}(n\lambda)\). Scaling by \(n\) produces \(\operatorname{Exp}(\lambda)\), exactly for every \(n\).

10. Uniform endpoint limit
Week 5Diagnostic

For iid \(U(0,1)\), find \(\lim_{n\to\infty}P(n(1-X_{(n)})>t)\) for \(t\ge0\).

Solution

The probability is \(P(X_{(n)}<1-t/n)=(1-t/n)^n\), which tends to \(e^{-t}\). The limit is exponential with rate 1.
