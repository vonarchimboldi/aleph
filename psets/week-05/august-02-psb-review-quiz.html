Questions 1-6 - Weekly ISI-Style Review
1. Method of indicators: isolated edges

In \(G(n,p)\), let \(X\) count edges that are present while every other edge incident to either endpoint is absent. Use indicators to find \(E[X]\). State why independence between different edge indicators is not needed for this expectation.

2. Conditional expectation: mixed Poisson count

Given \(\Lambda\), let \(X\sim\operatorname{Poisson}(\Lambda)\), where \(E[\Lambda]=a\) and \(\operatorname{Var}(\Lambda)=b\). Derive \(E[X]\) and \(\operatorname{Var}(X)\), naming both terms in the total-variance decomposition.

3. Order statistics: transformed maximum

Let \(F(x)=x^2\) on \(0<x<1\), and let \(M\) be the maximum of \(n\) iid observations. Derive the CDF and density of \(M\), including the complete support, then find the limiting survival function of \(n(1-M)\).

4. MLE: support and non-uniqueness

Observations are iid uniform on \([\theta-1,\theta+1]\). Write the likelihood support condition, identify the complete set of MLEs, and state when the observed sample has zero likelihood under every \(\theta\).

5. UMP/NP tests: exponential rate

For iid exponential observations, derive the likelihood-ratio direction for \(H_0:\lambda=\lambda_0\) versus \(H_1:\lambda=\lambda_1>\lambda_0\). Express the level-\(\alpha\) rejection region using \(\sum X_i\) and explain how its cutoff is calibrated.

6. Regression and OLS synthesis: constrained geometry

Minimize \(\|y-X\beta\|^2\) subject to \(R\beta=r\). Derive the Lagrange first-order equations and explain geometrically why the constrained residual need not be orthogonal to every column of \(X\).
