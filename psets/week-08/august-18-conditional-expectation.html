Core Pattern

Problems 1-5 - Concept Builders
1. Gamma-Poisson mean

If (Lambda\sim Gamma(a,b)) by rate and (X|\Lambda\sim Pois(\Lambda)), find (E[X]).

Solution

(E[X]=E[\Lambda]=a/b).

2. Gamma-Poisson variance

Find (Var(X)) in Problem 1.

Solution

(E[\Lambda]+Var(\Lambda)=a/b+a/b^2).

3. Best predictor

What minimizes (E[(X-g(Y))^2]) over functions (g)?

Solution

(g(Y)=E[X|Y]).

4. Random sum

For independent (N,Z_i), state (E[S_N]).

Solution

(E[N]E[Z_1]).

5. Conditional covariance identity

State the law of total covariance.

Solution

(Cov(X,Y)=E[Cov(X,Y|Z)]+Cov(E[X|Z],E[Y|Z])).

Problems 6-8 - Integration Problems
6. Shared random rate

Given (Lambda), let (X,Y) be independent (Pois(\Lambda)). Find (Cov(X,Y)).

Solution

Conditional covariance is zero; covariance of conditional means is (Var(\Lambda)).

7. Beta posterior prediction

With (P\sim Beta(a,b)) and (S|P\sim Bin(n,P)), find (P(Y=1|S=s)).

Solution

((a+s)/(a+b+n)).

8. Random-sum variance

State (Var(S_N)) for iid summands with mean (mu), variance (sigma^2).

Solution

(sigma^2E[N]+mu^2Var(N)).

Problems 9-10 - ISI-Style Challenge
9. Conditional orthogonality

Prove (X-E[X|Y]) is uncorrelated with every square-integrable function of (Y).

Solution

Condition the product on (Y); its conditional expectation is zero.

10. Variance reduction

Show (Var(E[X|Y])\le Var(X)).

Solution

Total variance writes (Var(X)=E[Var(X|Y)]+Var(E[X|Y])).

Answer Summary

Condition, compute the inner quantity, then apply tower, total variance, or total covariance.
