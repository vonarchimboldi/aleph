Core Pattern

Transform through the CDF when useful; otherwise count observations below, inside, and above the requested order points.

\[f_{k:n}(x)=\frac{n!}{(k-1)!(n-k)!}F(x)^{k-1}(1-F(x))^{n-k}f(x).\]
Problems 1-5 - Concept Builders
1. Uniform median

For five iid (U(0,1)) variables, identify the distribution and mean of (X_{(3)}).

Solution

(X_{(3)}\sim Beta(3,3)), with mean (1/2).

2. Exponential minimum

For iid (Exp(\lambda)) variables, find the distribution of (X_{(1)}).

Solution

The survival probability is (e^{-n\lambda x}), hence (X_{(1)}\sim Exp(n\lambda)).

3. Maximum density

For iid CDF (F) and density (f), derive the maximum density.

Solution

(P(X_{(n)}\le x)=F(x)^n), so the density is (nF(x)^{n-1}f(x)).

4. Uniform range mean

For (n) iid (U(0,1)), find (E[X_{(n)}-X_{(1)}]).

Solution

The endpoint means are (n/(n+1)) and (1/(n+1)), giving ((n-1)/(n+1)).

5. Quantile transform

If (U_{(k)}) is the (k)th uniform order statistic, express a general continuous (X_{(k)}).

Solution

(X_{(k)}\overset d=F^{-1}(U_{(k)})), with (U_{(k)}\sim Beta(k,n+1-k)).

Problems 6-8 - Integration Problems
6. Joint endpoints

Derive the joint density of minimum (U) and maximum (V) from (n) iid (U(0,1)).

Solution

Choose the endpoint observations and place the other (n-2) inside: (f_{U,V}(u,v)=n(n-1)(v-u)^{n-2}), (0<u<v<1).

7. Range density

Use Problem 6 to find the density of (R=V-U).

Solution

Integrate (u) from (0) to (1-r): (f_R(r)=n(n-1)r^{n-2}(1-r)), (0<r<1).

8. Exponential spacings

For iid (Exp(\lambda)), identify the distribution of (X_{(2)}-X_{(1)}).

Solution

After the first arrival, (n-1) exponential clocks remain; the spacing is (Exp((n-1)\lambda)), independent of the minimum.

Problems 9-10 - ISI-Style Challenge
9. Extreme-value scaling

For iid (Exp(1)), find the limit of (P(X_{(n)}-\log n\le x)).

Solution

([1-e^{-(x+\log n)}]^n=(1-e^{-x}/n)^n\to e^{-e^{-x}}), the Gumbel CDF.

10. Conditional interior points

Given uniform minimum (u) and maximum (v), describe the unordered remaining (n-2) sample points.

Solution

They are conditionally iid Uniform((u,v)); their ordered versions are the order statistics of that conditional sample.
