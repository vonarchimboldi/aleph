Core Pattern

State the ordered support first. Count observations below, at, between, and above the requested order statistics before simplifying.

\[f_{(k)}(x)=\frac{n!}{(k-1)!(n-k)!}F(x)^{k-1}[1-F(x)]^{n-k}f(x).\]
Problems 1-5 - Concept Builders
1. Kth uniform law
Order statisticBeta

Identify the distribution of \(U_{(k)}\) from \(n\) iid Uniform\((0,1)\) observations.

Solution

The kth order statistic has Beta\((k,n-k+1)\) density \(n!u^{k-1}(1-u)^{n-k}/[(k-1)!(n-k)!]\) on \(0<u<1\).

2. Expected kth uniform
Beta meanOrder statistic

Find \(E[U_{(k)}]\).

Solution

A Beta\((a,b)\) variable has mean \(a/(a+b)\). With \(a=k,b=n-k+1\), the answer is \(k/(n+1)\).

3. Maximum transform
CDF methodTransformation

If \(F(x)=x^3\) on \((0,1)\), find the CDF of the sample maximum.

Solution

All observations must be at most \(x\), so \(F_M(x)=F(x)^n=x^{3n}\) for \(0<x<1\), with the usual zero and one extensions outside the support.

4. Exponential spacings
SpacingExponential

For iid Exp\((\lambda)\), identify the law of \(X_{(1)}\) and of \(X_{(2)}-X_{(1)}\).

Solution

The minimum is Exp\((n\lambda)\). After the first event, memorylessness leaves \(n-1\) clocks, so the next spacing is Exp\(((n-1)\lambda)\), independent of the first spacing.

5. Min-max support
Joint supportMin/max

State the support of the minimum \(U\) and maximum \(V\) from a continuous distribution supported on \((a,b)\).

Solution

The ordered endpoints satisfy \(a<u<v<b\). This strict triangular support must accompany the joint density; outside it the density is zero.

Problems 6-8 - Integration Problems
6. General joint min-max law
Joint densityOrder statistics

For iid continuous observations with CDF \(F\) and density \(f\), find \(f_{U,V}(u,v)\).

Solution

Choose which observations attain the minimum and maximum, then place the other \(n-2\) between them. The density is \(n(n-1)[F(v)-F(u)]^{n-2}f(u)f(v)\) for \(u<v\).

7. Conditional interior points
Conditional lawUniform

For iid Uniform\((0,1)\), condition on minimum \(u\) and maximum \(v\). Describe the unordered remaining observations.

Solution

Given the endpoints, the remaining \(n-2\) observations are conditionally iid Uniform\((u,v)\). Their ordered versions are therefore uniform order statistics rescaled to that interval.

8. Probability integral transform
Quantile transformBeta

For continuous strictly increasing \(F\), identify the law of \(F(X_{(k)})\).

Solution

The transformed observations \(F(X_i)\) are iid Uniform\((0,1)\), and monotonicity preserves their order. Thus \(F(X_{(k)})\sim\operatorname{Beta}(k,n-k+1)\).

Problems 9-10 - ISI-Style Challenge
9. Uniform maximum endpoint limit
Scaling limitMaximum

For iid Uniform\((0,\theta)\), find the limiting survival function of \(n(\theta-X_{(n)})/\theta\).

Solution

For \(t\ge0\), the survival probability equals \(P(X_{(n)}<\theta(1-t/n))=(1-t/n)^n\to e^{-t}\). The scaled gap converges to Exp\((1)\).

10. Range density
TransformationRange

For \(n\) iid Uniform\((0,1)\), derive the density of the range \(R=X_{(n)}-X_{(1)}\).

Solution

Integrate the joint min-max density \(n(n-1)r^{n-2}\) over \(u\in(0,1-r)\). Thus \(f_R(r)=n(n-1)r^{n-2}(1-r)\) for \(0<r<1\).
