Core Pattern

Condition on the variable that freezes the random mechanism. Compute inside first, then average; for variance, keep both sources of randomness.

\[E[X]=E(E[X\mid Y]),\qquad \operatorname{Var}(X)=E(\operatorname{Var}(X\mid Y))+\operatorname{Var}(E[X\mid Y]).\]
Problems 1-5 - Concept Builders
1. Bernoulli mixture mean
Tower propertyMixture

Given \(P\), let \(X\mid P\sim\operatorname{Bernoulli}(P)\). Find \(E[X]\).

Solution

Conditionally, \(E[X\mid P]=P\). Applying the tower property gives \(E[X]=E[P]\). The entire mixing distribution affects other quantities, but only its mean is needed here.

2. Random-sum expectation
ConditioningRandom sum

Let \(S=Y_1+\cdots+Y_N\), where iid \(Y_i\) have mean \(\mu\) and are independent of \(N\). Find \(E[S]\).

Solution

Given \(N\), the sum has mean \(N\mu\). Average again to obtain \(E[S]=\mu E[N]\), assuming the relevant expectations are finite.

3. Poisson mixture variance
Total varianceMixed Poisson

If \(X\mid\Lambda\sim\operatorname{Poisson}(\Lambda)\), find \(\operatorname{Var}(X)\).

Solution

Both conditional mean and variance equal \(\Lambda\). Therefore \(\operatorname{Var}(X)=E[\Lambda]+\operatorname{Var}(\Lambda)\), separating counting noise from variation in the random rate.

4. Best predictor
Conditional meanPrediction

Among all functions \(g(Y)\), which minimizes \(E[(X-g(Y))^2]\)?

Solution

The minimizer is \(g(Y)=E[X\mid Y]\). The orthogonal decomposition splits the error into irreducible conditional variance plus the squared distance from the conditional mean.

5. Fair-game increment
Conditional expectationMartingale cue

If \(E[X_{k+1}\mid X_0,\ldots,X_k]=X_k\), find \(E[X_{k+1}-X_k]\).

Solution

The conditional expected increment is zero. Taking expectations once more gives \(E[X_{k+1}-X_k]=0\), provided integrability holds.

Problems 6-8 - Integration Problems
6. Beta-Bernoulli prediction
Posterior predictionBeta

Let \(P\sim\operatorname{Beta}(a,b)\), and conditionally let \(X_1,\ldots,X_n\) be iid Bernoulli(\(P\)). After \(s\) successes, find \(P(X_{n+1}=1\mid X_1,\ldots,X_n)\).

Solution

The posterior is Beta\((a+s,b+n-s)\). The predictive success probability is its mean, \((a+s)/(a+b+n)\).

7. Random-sum variance
Total varianceRandom sum

In Problem 2, suppose \(\operatorname{Var}(Y_i)=\sigma^2\). Find \(\operatorname{Var}(S)\).

Solution

Given \(N\), variance is \(N\sigma^2\) and mean is \(N\mu\). Total variance yields \(\sigma^2E[N]+\mu^2\operatorname{Var}(N)\).

8. Conditional covariance
Total covarianceCommon environment

Given \(P\), let \(X,Y\) be conditionally independent Bernoulli(\(P\)). Find \(\operatorname{Cov}(X,Y)\).

Solution

Conditional covariance is zero, while both conditional means equal \(P\). The total-covariance identity gives \(\operatorname{Cov}(X,Y)=\operatorname{Var}(P)\); shared randomness creates positive marginal dependence.

Problems 9-10 - ISI-Style Challenge
9. Geometric stopping cost
Stopping countConditioning

Independent trials succeed with random probability \(P\), fixed after being drawn. Given \(P\), let \(N\) be the trial of first success. Express \(E[N]\), and state when it is finite.

Solution

Conditionally, \(E[N\mid P]=1/P\). Hence \(E[N]=E[1/P]\), not \(1/E[P]\). It is finite precisely when the reciprocal is integrable.

10. Variance reduction by information
Conditional varianceInformation

Show that \(E[\operatorname{Var}(X\mid Y)]\le\operatorname{Var}(X)\), and identify the equality condition.

Solution

Total variance writes \(\operatorname{Var}(X)\) as the requested term plus \(\operatorname{Var}(E[X\mid Y])\), which is nonnegative. Equality holds exactly when \(E[X\mid Y]\) is almost surely constant.
