Questions 1-6 - Weekly ISI-Style Review
1. Indicators: occupied boxes and covariance
IndicatorsPair products

Throw \(m\) balls independently and uniformly into \(n\) boxes. Let \(X\) be the number of occupied boxes. Derive \(E[X]\). Then express \(\operatorname{Var}(X)\) using one-box and two-box occupancy probabilities, simplifying those probabilities explicitly.

2. Conditional expectation: beta-binomial prediction
Tower propertyTotal variance

Let \(P\sim\operatorname{Beta}(a,b)\), and given \(P\), let \(X\sim\operatorname{Bin}(n,P)\). Derive \(E[X]\) and \(\operatorname{Var}(X)\) using conditional expectation and total variance. Identify the conditional-noise and mixing-variance terms.

3. Order statistics: conditional point inside the range
Joint order lawConditional distribution

Let \(X_1,X_2,X_3\) be iid Uniform\((0,1)\). Derive the joint density of \(U=X_{(1)}\), \(V=X_{(3)}\). Then find the conditional density of the middle order statistic \(X_{(2)}\) given \(U=u,V=v\).

4. Estimation: Rao–Blackwell and UMVUE
SufficiencyRao–BlackwellUMVUE

For iid Poisson\((\lambda)\) observations, start with \(T=I\{X_1=0\}\), an unbiased estimator of \(e^{-\lambda}\). Condition on \(S=\sum_iX_i\) to derive an improved estimator. State the completeness/sufficiency fact that makes it the UMVUE.

5. UMP testing: exponential rate boundary
UMPSize and power

For iid Exp\((\lambda)\) observations, construct the level-\(\alpha\) UMP test of \(H_0:\lambda\le\lambda_0\) against \(H_1:\lambda>\lambda_0\). Derive the rejection direction, state the null-boundary calibration distribution, and write the power function.

6. Regression repair: constrained normal equations
Week 5 repairConstrained OLSResidual geometry

Let \(X\) have full column rank. Minimize \(\|y-X\beta\|^2\) subject to \(R\beta=r\). Derive the block first-order system and the closed-form constrained estimator in terms of unrestricted \(\hat\beta\). Explain precisely which feasible directions remain orthogonal to the constrained residual and why \(X^Te_c\) need not be zero.
