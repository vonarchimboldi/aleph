R2-DSA-03-T-Q1 · delayed

For n a power of two, compare T(n)=2T(n/2)+n with U(n)=U(n−1)+n, with constant base costs. Explain the sums.

Worked check · open after attempting

T has log 2 n levels with Θ(n) work per level: Θ(n log n). U sums n+(n−1)+…+1: Θ(n²).

R2-DSA-03-T-Q2 · transfer

The two-branch function above is evaluated at n=4 without memoization. Give total calls and maximum simultaneously active calls, including the initial and base calls.

Worked check · open after attempting

C(4)=1+9+5=15 total. A longest chain is 4,3,2,1,0, so maximum active calls=5.

Big picture

A recurrence must count the exact quantity asked for; output value, total calls, depth and expected work are different objects.

Name the counted primitive. → Expand a tiny call tree including base-case calls. → Separate sequential branches from simultaneous stack depth. → For expected cost, average over the stated random choice.

  • Counting only non-base calls
  • Reporting depth when asked for total activations
