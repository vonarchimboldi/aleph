R2-DBMS-06-T-Q1 · delayed

Only (A,x),(A,y),(B,x) occur in a fact table. How many rows does CUBE(D1,D2) return? Contrast the complete-product shortcut.

Worked check · open after attempting

3 detail groups+2 D1 groups+2 D2 groups+1 grand total=8. The complete-product shortcut would incorrectly count a nonexistent (B,y) detail group.

R2-DBMS-06-T-Q2 · transfer

One subgroup has average sales 10 over 2 facts; another has average 20 over 8 facts. Find the combined average and explain the information needed for a roll-up.

Worked check · open after attempting

(10×2+20×8)/(2+8)=18. Retain sums and counts; the unweighted average 15 is wrong.

Big picture

Count choices of aggregation level separately from rows inside a cuboid; drilling down changes resolution rather than filtering an arbitrary subset.

State one fact row’s grain. → Name the hierarchy level before and after an operation. → Count cuboids by independent level choices. → For CUBE row counts, sum the sizes of each grouping set.

  • Adding averages without group sizes
