R2-DSA-06-T-Q1 · delayed

A rooted full binary tree has 5 internal nodes. Derive its number of leaves and total nodes using edges.

Worked check · open after attempting

There are 2I edges, also I+L−1 edges; L=I+1=6 and N=11.

R2-DSA-06-T-Q2 · transfer

With height counted in edges, give minimum and maximum node counts for a binary tree of height 3. Exhibit shapes attaining them.

Worked check · open after attempting

Minimum 4: one root-to-leaf chain. Maximum 15: a complete tree with all levels 0–3 filled. Structural bounds, not a BST ordering fact.

Big picture

Traversal sequences encode structural constraints; uniqueness requires the right assumptions, not just a memorized pair of traversals.

Locate the root using preorder or postorder. → Partition the inorder sequence into left and right subtrees. → Recurse on the corresponding subsequences. → Check each structural claim against the reconstructed tree or a counterexample.

Use the error to choose the next task

  • Confusing a binary tree with a BST
  • Assuming preorder alone determines shape
  • Applying full-tree equalities to an arbitrary binary tree
