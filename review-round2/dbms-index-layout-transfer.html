R2-DBMS-05-T-Q1 · delayed

Leaf capacity 2; internal capacity 3 children. Separators are minimum keys of right children. Root[5,9] has leaves[1,3],[5,7],[9,11]. Worked check · open after attempting

Left leaf splits to[1] and[2,3], adding separator 2. Old root becomes[2,5,9] with 4 children, so split it and promote 5. New root[5], left internal[2], right internal[9].

R2-DBMS-05-T-Q2 · transfer

Same 256-byte internal node as before, but reserve a16-byte header. Recompute fanout and explain whether a12-byte record pointer belongs in the inequality.

Worked check · open after attempting

8p+16(p−1)+16≤256 gives 24p≤256, so p=10. Record pointers belong to leaves in the stated model, not internal child-pointer slots.
