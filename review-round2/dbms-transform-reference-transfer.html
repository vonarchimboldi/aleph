R2-DBMS-07-T-Q2 · transfer

Transform x using z=(x−μ)/σ with known σ>0. Can x be recovered? Compare with replacing x by its interval label.

Worked check · open after attempting

Yes: x=μ+σz if parameters and precision are retained. A bin label usually loses within-bin location. Discretization/compression extensions are syllabus-driven; the direct observed paper anchor here is z-score normalization.

Big picture

A transformation’s name, reference parameters and information-loss assumptions determine what can be computed and what can be recovered.
