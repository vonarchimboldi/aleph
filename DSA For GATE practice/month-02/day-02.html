Overview: queues and deques

Session budget

Build the primitive

Implement a fixed-capacity ring queue with head and size; derive tail=(head+size)%capacity.

Apply it

Replay FIFO requests in a ring buffer; trace both-end deque operations without requiring a second full implementation.

Explain why it works

Logical item i lives at (head+i)%capacity; 0<=size<=capacity.

Analyze the costs

O(1) worst-case endpoint operations, O(capacity) allocated storage; no list.pop(0).

Test the boundaries

Full vs empty, wrap-around, capacity one, invalid operation contract.
