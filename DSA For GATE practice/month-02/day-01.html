Explain why it works

The live prefix is exactly the stack, and its final entry is the top.

Analyze the costs

Push: O(1) amortized, O(n) on a resize; pop/peek O(1). Capacity is O(n) for insertion-only growth; after many pops it tracks historical maximum size.

Test the boundaries

Empty pop, singleton, duplicates, several growth boundaries.
