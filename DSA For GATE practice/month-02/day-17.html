Killer application: top-k in a stream

Session budget

0–10 recall · 10–25 baseline and choice · 25–45 primitive · 45–70 application and tests · 70–85 analysis/GATE · 85–90 exit note

One main task. Stop at 90 minutes and record partial work; optional extensions stay within the session.

Build the primitive

Maintain a size-k min-heap using Day 5 operations; heapq is permitted after the foundational implementation.

Apply it

Maintain the kth largest observation as values arrive, counting duplicate observations.

Explain why it works

Once k values exist, the heap contains k largest values seen; its root is the kth largest.

Analyze the costs

O(log k) accepted update, O(1) rejected comparison/peek; O(n log(k+1)) total upper bound and O(k) storage.

Test the boundaries

k=1, fewer than k values, duplicates, negatives, a long rejected tail.

GATE practice

Original MCQ: why a min-heap, not a max-heap, is the correct retained frontier.
